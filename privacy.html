Privacy Policy

Plain version: Finonda helps you track your household spending. Your financial data is stored in India, is never sold or used for advertising, and you can see, correct, or delete it at any time. The sections below are the full detail, written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

Effective date: 18 October 2026 · Applies to the Finonda mobile app, web app, and finonda.com.

1. Who we are

Finonda is operated by FINONDA TECHNOLOGIES (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED ("Finonda", "we", "us"), the data fiduciary for the personal data described here. You can reach us about anything in this policy — including any privacy question, request, or grievance — at support@finonda.com.

2. What we collect

  • Account details — your email address, and a display name and profile details you choose to add.
  • Financial information you enter or scan — expenses, income, budgets, bills, warranties, service records, and the line items, amounts, merchants and dates within them.
  • Receipt images — when you scan a bill, the photo is sent for reading and only the extracted details are kept; see section 4.
  • Household ("Nest") information — the Nests you create or join and their members, so shared expenses work.
  • Technical & diagnostic data — limited device and error information used to keep the app reliable (see section 5).
  • Waitlist — if you joined the waitlist, we hold your email address and any quiz answers you gave, and nothing else, until the app launches.

We do not ask for government ID numbers, and we do not link your bank or payment accounts.

3. Why we use it (purposes)

  • To provide the core service — recording, organising and showing your household spending.
  • To read receipts you scan and pre-fill expense details for you.
  • To power optional spending insights, only if you turn them on.
  • To keep the app secure and reliable, and to respond to your support requests.
  • To improve the product using aggregated, non-identifying trends.

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent, which you give when you create an account and agree to this policy. You can withdraw consent at any time (section 7); withdrawing it may mean parts of the app no longer work.

4. Receipt scanning & AI

When you scan a receipt or use "smart add", the image or text is sent to Google Gemini (operated by Google LLC) purely to extract the details — the merchant, amounts, items and dates. Finonda keeps only those extracted details. We do not store the receipt image after it has been read, and under our agreement with Google your content is not used to train Google's models. This processing happens on Google's servers, which are outside India (see section 6).

6. Cross-border transfers

Your core data is stored in India (Supabase, AWS Mumbai). Some processing involves transfers outside India — receipt/text reading by Google Gemini (United States) and error reporting via Sentry. We make these transfers to deliver and maintain the service, limit what is sent, and the DPDP Act permits transfers to countries not restricted by the Central Government.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct or update your data — most of it you can edit directly in the app.
  • Erase your data — Profile → Delete Account, or write to us.
  • Withdraw consent — including turning off optional spending insights in the app at any time.
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Grievance redressal — raise a complaint and have it addressed (section 9).

To make any of these requests, use the in-app options or write to support@finonda.com.

8. How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you request deletion, the actual purge is scheduled 14 days after your request (a grace period during which you can cancel and recover your account). After the purge, your personal data is removed from our active systems, and copies in encrypted backups age out of rotation shortly afterwards. Aggregated, non-identifying information may be retained.

9. Grievance redressal

If you have a complaint about how your personal data is handled, write to us at support@finonda.com with the details. We will acknowledge and work to resolve your grievance within the timelines required under the DPDP Act and its rules.

10. Children

Finonda is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit and at rest, strict per-household access controls, and an optional app lock on your device. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your information safe and to limit who can access it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves or the law changes. We will post the new version here with a new effective date, and notify you in the app of material changes.
