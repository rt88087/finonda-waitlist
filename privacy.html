Privacy Policy

Plain version: Finonda is a household management app that helps you keep your expenses, warranties, services, reminders and budgets organised in one place. This policy explains what we collect, why, who processes it, and the rights you have — wherever you live. We never sell your data or use it for advertising, and you can see, correct or delete it at any time.

Effective date: 12 November 2026 · Applies to the Finonda mobile app, web app, and finonda.com.

1. Who we are

Finonda is operated by FINONDA TECHNOLOGIES (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED ("Finonda", "we", "us"), which is the controller / data fiduciary for the personal data described here. You can reach us about anything in this policy — including any privacy question, request or complaint — at support@finonda.com. Our Grievance Officer is named in section 11.

2. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to everyone who uses Finonda, wherever they are. Depending on where you live, specific laws give you additional rights, and we honour them — including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act); the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR); and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended (CCPA/CPRA). Your region-specific rights are in section 8.

3. What we collect

  • Account details — your email address, and a display name and profile details you choose to add.
  • The records and transactions you enter or scan — your household entries such as expenses, service reminders, reminders, warranties and budgets, and the line items, amounts, merchants and dates within them.
  • Receipt images — when you scan a bill, the photo is sent for reading and only the extracted details are kept; see section 4.
  • Household ("Nest") information — the Nests you create or join and their members, so shared entries work.
  • Technical & diagnostic data — limited device and error information used to keep the app reliable (see section 5).
  • Waitlist — if you joined the waitlist, we hold your email address and any quiz answers you gave, and nothing else, until the app launches.

We do not ask for government ID numbers, and we do not connect to your bank or payment accounts.

4. Receipt scanning & AI

When you scan a receipt or use "smart add", the image or text is sent to Google Gemini (operated by Google LLC) purely to extract the details — the merchant, amounts, items and dates. Finonda keeps only those extracted details. We do not store the receipt image after it has been read, and under our agreement with Google your content is not used to train Google's models. This processing happens on Google's servers outside your country (see section 7).

5. Why we use it, and our legal bases

  • To provide the core service — recording, organising and showing your household entries.
  • To read receipts you scan and pre-fill details for you.
  • To power optional insights, only if you turn them on.
  • To keep the app secure and reliable, and to respond to your support requests.
  • To improve the product using aggregated, non-identifying trends.

Where the DPDP Act applies, we process your personal data on the basis of your consent, given when you create an account and agree to this policy, and withdrawable at any time (section 8). Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are: performance of a contract (to provide the service you ask for), consent (for optional features such as insights and for AI receipt reading), and our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure, reliable and improving it. Withdrawing consent may mean parts of the app no longer work.

7. International transfers

Your core data is stored in India (Supabase, AWS Mumbai). Some processing involves transfers to the United States — receipt/text reading by Google Gemini and error reporting via Sentry. Where the GDPR applies, such transfers are made under appropriate safeguards (such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses) or because the transfer is necessary to provide the service you requested. Where the DPDP Act applies, transfers are permitted to countries not restricted by the Central Government. We limit what is sent in every case.

8. Your rights

You can always access a summary of your data, correct most of it directly in the app, erase it (Profile → Delete Account, or by writing to us), withdraw consent (including turning off optional insights), and export your data. Depending on where you live you also have:

India (DPDP Act)

  • Access, correction and erasure of your personal data.
  • Withdrawal of consent at any time.
  • Nomination of another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Grievance redressal (section 11).

EEA & UK (GDPR)

  • Access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, and data portability.
  • Objection to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdrawal of consent.
  • The right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA)

  • The right to know, access, correct and delete the personal information we hold.
  • The right to opt out of "sale" or "sharing" of personal information — we do not sell or share your personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of.
  • The right to limit use of sensitive personal information, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

9. How to exercise your rights

Use the in-app options, or write to support@finonda.com. We will verify your request and respond within the timelines required by the law that applies to you. Exercising your rights is free, and we will not treat you differently for doing so.

10. How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you request deletion, the actual purge is scheduled 14 days after your request (a grace period during which you can cancel and recover your account). After the purge, your personal data is removed from our active systems, and copies in encrypted backups age out of rotation shortly afterwards. Aggregated, non-identifying information may be retained.

11. Grievance & complaints

If you have a complaint about how your personal data is handled, contact our Grievance Officer, Rosamma Thomas, at support@finonda.com with the details. We will acknowledge and work to resolve your grievance within the timelines required by applicable law. EEA/UK users may also complain to their local supervisory authority; India users may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

12. Data breaches

We maintain safeguards to protect your data. In the unlikely event of a personal-data breach that affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities where and as required by applicable law, and take reasonable steps to contain and remedy it. This notice describes our process and does not amount to an admission of fault or liability; any limitations of our liability are set out in our Terms of Service.

13. Children

Finonda is intended only for users aged 18 and over, and we ask you to confirm your age when you sign up. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit and at rest, strict per-household access controls, and an optional app lock on your device. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your information safe and to limit who can access it.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves or the law changes. We will post the new version here with a new effective date, and notify you in the app of material changes.
